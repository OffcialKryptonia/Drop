Your journey deserves confidence.

What DROP does for safety today — stated plainly, and only what is actually implemented.

Before you ride

  • Drivers are reviewed before they drive. Every driver application — personal details, vehicle information and documents — is checked by the DROP team. Only approved drivers in good standing can accept trips.
  • Documents stay checked. Going online verifies that no required document has expired, and that the driver wallet meets the minimum balance.

During your trip

  • Follow the trip live. Passengers see the driver's live position in the app while a trip is active; drivers follow the trip to pickup and destination.
  • SOS alerts with trip context. Raise an alert from the app during a trip. DROP saves it with your trip, vehicle and location details for the operations team to see in the admin panel, with email alerts where configured.
  • Share your trip. Passengers can share a link so someone they trust can follow the journey.

After your trip

  • Trip history. Trip details stay in your account so you can look back at any journey.
  • Report problems. Raise a complaint from the app and receive an admin reply, or contact us directly by email or phone.

What SOS is — and is not

SOS alerts are saved for the DROP operations team with your trip context, visible in the admin panel, with email alerts sent where configured (best effort). SOS does not place a call to the police, ambulance or any emergency service. If you are in immediate danger, contact your local emergency services first, then tell us.

Ratings and reviews are coming next and will add another layer of accountability. We will update this page as new safety features go live.
