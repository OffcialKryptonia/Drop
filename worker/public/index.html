Technology that keeps trips moving.

DROP is a technology platform first: fares are computed on the server, trips are coordinated digitally, and maps can be switched without an app update.

  • Server-computed faresDistance, time and ride type are priced on the server for every trip — the phone never sets the price.
  • Live trip coordinationRequests reach nearby eligible drivers in the app, with push alerts where configured; the first to accept gets the trip.
  • Maps that adaptGoogle Maps or OpenStreetMap, configured centrally and picked up by the app automatically.
  • Secure accountsSign-in with secure authentication tokens, password reset by emailed code, and role-gated driver mode.

More about DROP

Questions, answered.

Only what the app and platform do today. Where something is not live yet, we say so.

What is DROP?

DROP is a Nigerian mobility platform connecting passengers and approved drivers through one app, with server-computed fares, digital trip coordination and wallet or cash payments.

How does DROP work?

You tell the app where you’re going, see the fare before you confirm, and a nearby eligible driver accepts your request. You follow the trip in the app, then pay and find the trip in your history.

Is DROP one app for passengers and drivers?

Yes. Passenger mode is the default. Approved drivers switch to driver mode in the same app — there is no separate download.

How do I create a passenger account?

Open the DROP app, sign up with your name, phone number and email, and verify your account. The apps for Android and iPhone are coming soon.

How do I become a DROP driver?

In the app, start a driver application: add your personal details, vehicle information and a profile photo, upload the required documents, then submit for review. Start on the driver page.

What documents do drivers need?

The app lists the required documents for your region when you apply. Going online later also checks that no required document has expired.

How are drivers approved?

The DROP team reviews each application and its documents, and can approve, request changes, reject or suspend. Only approved drivers in good standing can switch to driver mode and go online.

How do I request a ride?

Open the app, enter your destination, check the estimated fare, and confirm. Immediate rides only — scheduled rides are not live yet.

How do I know my ride fare?

The app shows a server-computed estimate before you confirm, based on distance, time and ride type. The phone never sets the price.

What payment methods does DROP support?

Ride fares settle by DROP wallet (topped up by NGN bank transfer to your own virtual account) or cash paid to the driver. In-app card and saved-card payments are not operational; card checkout is available only through PayMyRide links shared for a trip, where configured.

Can I track my journey?

Yes. Passengers see the driver's live position in the app while a trip is active, and trip details stay in your history.

What happens if I need to cancel a ride?

You can cancel from the app. A cancellation fee may apply once a driver has accepted — for example after the driver arrives or a grace period passes — and it is taken from your wallet balance where available.

How can I contact support?

Email contact@drop.taxi, call 0808 606 0986, raise a complaint from inside the app, or use the SOS option during a trip if you need urgent help.

Where does DROP currently operate?

DROP is being built for Nigeria first, region by region. We will announce launch cities here once they are confirmed — open the app to check availability in your area.

How does DROP handle user information?

Account details, location while you use the apps, trip and payment records, and — for drivers — vehicle details and approval documents. See the privacy policy (draft) and terms (draft). Final versions will be published before public launch.
