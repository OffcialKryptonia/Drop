Questions, answered.

How does DROP work?

You tell the DROP Passenger app where you’re going, a nearby driver using the DROP Driver app accepts your request, and you ride. Payment and rating happen in the app.

How do I request a ride?

Open the app, enter your destination, check the estimated fare, and confirm. You can also schedule a ride for later.

How do I become a driver?

Install the DROP Driver app, create an account and add your vehicle and documents. Start on the driver page.

Are drivers verified?

Driver registrations, including documents, are reviewed by the DROP team before a driver can accept trips. We will publish more detail about our checks as the service launches.

How can I pay?

You can pay with your DROP wallet or with cash. We are adding more ways to pay, and the app will show what is available to you.

How do I contact support?

Email contact@drop.taxi or call 0808 606 0986. You can also raise a complaint from inside the app.

Where can I get the app?

The apps are coming soon to Google Play and the App Store. We will add the download links here as soon as they are live.

Where does DROP operate?

DROP is being built for Nigeria first. We will announce launch cities here once they are confirmed.
