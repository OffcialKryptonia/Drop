A mobility company built for Nigerian cities.

DROP exists to make getting around simpler for passengers — and fairer for the drivers who move them.

What we are building

One platform with two connected components: a single mobile app with passenger and driver modes, and an operations platform that keeps trips running. Passengers set a destination, see a server-computed fare before confirming, and follow the driver's live position. Approved drivers switch to driver mode, go online, and accept nearby requests.

How we work

  • Clear fares. Every fare is computed on the server from distance, time and ride type — the phone never sets the price.
  • Reliable matching. Requests reach nearby eligible drivers; the first to accept gets the trip.
  • Honest rollout. Features go live in stages. Where something is not ready — scheduled rides, ratings, in-app card payments — we say so instead of advertising it.

Where we operate

DROP is being built for Nigeria first, region by region. Launch cities will be announced on the home page once confirmed.
