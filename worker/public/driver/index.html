Drive with DROP.

Registration happens in the DROP Driver app. Install it, create your account, and add your vehicle and documents.

What you will need

  • A smartphone running the DROP Driver app
  • Your vehicle details
  • Your driving documents, ready to upload in the app

What happens next

The DROP team reviews your registration and documents. Once you are approved, you can start accepting ride requests, and track your earnings and trip history in the app.
